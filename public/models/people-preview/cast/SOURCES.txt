Bite Tycoon — 100-person visual prototype

Source: MakeHuman core human mesh, morph targets and system assets (CC0).
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip

Generated with MPFB 2.0.6-rc2 and Blender 4.2.23 LTS.
The GPL software licenses for these tools do not change the CC0 license
of the exported core MakeHuman characters and bundled system assets.

100 individually configured faces and bodies: 50 adult customers, 30 child
visitors aged 5–15, and 20 adult staff. manifest.json records outfits, hairstyles and a mesh
signature for each person. These are generated characters, not scanned
Renderpeople models. Renderpeople assets in the parent folder have a
different license, documented in the parent SOURCES.txt.

All models are rigged. Motions, trays, hats and other role accessories
in this preview are original procedural demonstrations, not mocap clips.
Portraits and full-outfit images show the actual exported models. All outfits
use audited long-sleeved shirts/jackets and full-length trousers with closed
shoes, fitted to each body. The asset names begin with "male" because that is
the source pack's naming; MPFB fits these garments to all selected body shapes.
No short skirts, shorts or crop tops are included in this cast. Some service
uniforms use simple new material colors on the same fitted clothing meshes.

This page is a separate visual prototype. No live game characters,
management behavior or saved progress are replaced.
