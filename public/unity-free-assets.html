What this shortlist means

These are store listings, not assets already installed or benchmarked in your game. Preview images belong to their respective publishers and are loaded from Unity’s image servers. Store artwork may use lighting that differs from your game.

Browser suitability above is my assessment of the published information. We still need to check materials, model complexity, texture memory and frame rate on a test street. No complete free airport terminal was confirmed in this shortlist; the aircraft option only adds aircraft.

Each linked listing identifies its license. Check that license and the current free price when adding the asset to your Unity account. The existing city remains the working game while you compare these options.
